
SuperAmin
Can create,read ,update and delete Schools

SchoolAdmin, School Staff member
Can create,read ,update and delete Deparments
Can create,read ,update and delete Classes
Can create,read ,update and delete Teachers
Can create,read ,update and delete Students
Can create,read ,update and delete Courses
Can create,read ,update and delete Invoices
Can send messages to Teachers and Students


Students
A Student can take one or more Courses,
Qualifications for those students,
A Student can have one on more Addresses,
A student can create a study plan to prepare for upcoming exams.

Teachers,
Can create,read ,update and delete Homework
A Teacher can plan a Parent-Teacher meeting for their Students,
A Teacher can have one on more Addresses,
can send messages to Students and Parents,
Can create documents for their Students, such as a documents for a specific lessen or a document for a specific course 
or a document for a specific exam,
or a document for a specific homework assignment,
or a document for a specific Parent-Teacher meeting,
or a document for an overview of exam marks

Courses
A Course can be given remote or in a specific ClassRoom,

Exams
A Exam can be associated with one or more Courses,
A Exam can be associated with one or more Students,
A Exam can be associated with one Teachers,
A Exam can be of the type QuestionAndAnswer or MultipleChoice

A school consists of
Departments, which have Classes, which have Students and Teachers.

Invoices
A student may have outstanding invoices, for example,
due to enrolling in a specific course.
